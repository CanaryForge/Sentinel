An escape is not an event. It is a ladder, and most runs climb all the way.

Every run is scored from level 0, no signal at all, to level 6, sandbox broken and the heartbeat gone. Reading the corpus as a ladder rather than a pass or fail rate shows where the agent stalls, where it accelerates, and which monitor is awake at each rung. Detection itself is almost always instant: the median lands in 7 milliseconds, and the one-second average is the work of a handful of slow outliers.
